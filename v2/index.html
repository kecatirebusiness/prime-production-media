The story behind

Why I do this?

I've spent more than a decade building businesses in industries that had nothing in common. Different products, different markets, different rules.

And during that whole time I saw how completely different projects ended up needing the same pieces, the same rules, the same logic.

Until at some point I realized that of all of them, those that broke, in the moment of growth, needed exactly the same thing.

The same four gears. In the same order. Built the same way.

That discovery completely changed the way I built businesses. And I spent years thinking that some day I'd tell it openly, because most founders who know me ask me exactly the same thing: how did I explain in four steps what they had taken years to learn or ever understand.

PRIME is exactly that. The day where your business stops improvising and starts working with the structure it needed from day one.
