The story behind

Why I do this.

I've spent more than a decade building businesses in industries that had nothing in common. Different products, different markets, different rules — yet every time the brand actually scaled, the same pattern repeated.

The same four gears. In the same order. Built the same way.

"Strategy. Production. Media. AI. In that order — every single time."

The brands that broke had one thing in common too: the chain. A strategy agency that didn't shoot. A production house that didn't run ads. A media buyer who never saw the brief. Three vendors, zero accountability.

The system

The 4 steps.

Every brand we touch runs through this pipeline. Same order. Every time. No exceptions.

01

Strategy.

Funnel first. We start with the business goal, not the moodboard. Positioning, audience, offer and KPIs — locked before we touch a camera.

02

Production.

In-house studio. Direction, cinema, edit, motion. From a single UGC reel to a 30-spot campaign. We shoot it. We cut it. We deliver it.

03

Media.

Meta, TikTok, YouTube. Daily optimization, weekly creative refresh. Test 18 hooks, keep 3, scale the winner. If it doesn't move CAC, we kill it.

04

AI Creative.

Generative video, image and copy at scale. Avatars, product shots, variant hooks. Ten ad versions before lunch — not next quarter.
